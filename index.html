About Us

Welcome to Design Edutech

Since 19 June 2000, Design Edutech has helped students in Ranjhi start their careers in computers. We are a study centre of Makhanlal Chaturvedi University, Bhopal, offering university diploma and degree courses in computer applications.

Our 8,000 sq ft campus has a 50-computer lab, a digital classroom and a library of more than 5,000 books. Our students are known for some of the best PGDCA and DCA results in Jabalpur, and many now hold senior posts in India and abroad.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions students and parents ask us most. For anything else, call or WhatsApp us.

Still have questions? Call us0761-2430668
Which university are the courses affiliated with?
Our diploma and degree courses are offered through Makhanlal Chaturvedi National University of Journalism and Communication, Bhopal. Design Edutech is a study centre of the university, and the university awards the diploma or degree.
What is the eligibility for DCA and PGDCA?
DCA is for students who have passed 12th in any stream. PGDCA is for graduates of any stream. Both are one-year courses with two semesters.
Can I do M.Sc. (CS) after PGDCA?
Yes. PGDCA holders can join M.Sc. (CS) through lateral entry, directly in the second year.
Is Tally training included?
Yes. Tally and computer hardware training are free for students enrolled in our university courses.
What are the fees?
Fees depend on the course and the session. Call 0761-2430668 or visit the institute for the current fee structure.
What are the office timings?
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The institute is closed on Sunday.
